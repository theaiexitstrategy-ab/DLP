## 0. The most important finding: Google may not know the site exists

A search for "Daniels' Legacy Planning" and for "danielslegacyplanning.com" returned nothing from the site (checked). A site that doesn't show up for its own name can't win any local search. On top of that, the site has:

- no `sitemap.xml` or `robots.txt`
- no schema.org markup on any page
- no canonical link and no meta description on the home page
- no phone number in the page text
- no mention of Medicare anywhere, even though Medicare is a target product

Phase 1 fixes that foundation before any new content goes up.

## 1. The search landscape

### Lawton and the surrounding hour

- **Who shows up (checked):**
  - Company-owned agent pages: about 10 State Farm agents in Lawton, plus Shelter.
  - Directory sites: BBB category pages, medicareagentshub.com (with separate pages for Lawton, Fort Sill, Duncan, Frederick and Cache), and thin lead-generation directories.
  - A handful of independent agencies:
    - Brooks Insurance Agency (life, Medicare and annuities)
    - Champions United Financial Services
    - ABC Medicare & Health Insurance Agency
    - Angela Schertle Agency
    - HealthMarkets agents
- **Their weaknesses (checked):**
  - State Farm and Shelter pages are templates. Only one Lawton State Farm agent mentions final expense, and none say anything specific about Lawton.
  - The directories have no real content.
  - We found no independent Lawton agency with a real page about final expense or mortgage protection.
- **Competition: low to medium (est.).** A page with real local substance from an independent agent should be able to reach page one for searches about final expense, mortgage protection and life insurance in Lawton within about 90 days. Medicare is the most crowded topic, because of directory sites and AEP-season advertisers.
- **Fort Sill (checked against VA sources):**
  - Departing service members can convert SGLI to VGLI **with no health questions if they apply within 240 days of separation**. After that, health questions apply, up to 1 year and 120 days after separation.
  - SGLI coverage continues free for 120 days after separation.
  - That makes "should I keep VGLI or buy private coverage?" a real and timely question that no local competitor answers. Be careful in how it's written: VGLI is a government benefit, so the page has to stay neutral and educational, and never tell anyone to drop VGLI. (verify the wording with Anthony)
- **Towns within about an hour of Lawton (est. drive times):**

### St. Louis metro, Missouri side

- **Who shows up (checked):**
  - Heavy company-owned agent presence: State Farm (dozens of agents), American Family and Shelter.
  - Independent agencies in St. Charles: Webb Insurance, HSGW Insurance Services, Brightway, plus HealthMarkets.
- **The gap:**
  - No local page clearly aimed at **mortgage protection** appeared in the St. Louis results (checked).
  - Final expense is mentioned by only one State Farm agent's page (checked).
- **Competition: medium to high (est.).**
  - Broad searches like "life insurance St. Louis" are hard to win.
  - Product searches (final expense, mortgage protection) and suburb-plus-product searches are winnable.
  - Don't build separate suburb pages until Search Console shows demand for them. Cloned city pages are against Google's rules on doorway pages.

### Google Business Profile: the rule that shapes everything (checked, Google's help pages)

- A business with no storefront where customers visit must hide its address and list a service area.
- Service areas are limited to 20 cities or ZIP codes, and the whole area should be within about 2 hours' drive of the base.
- **Lawton and St. Louis are about 7 hours apart, so one profile can't honestly cover both.** A second profile is allowed only if there is a real location in that market, staffed during posted hours.
- Insurance agents count as individual practitioners. A solo agent should use one profile, named in the "Daniels' Legacy Planning" or "[company]: [person]" style (verify the exact naming against Google's current guidelines).
- The decision for Anthony (§9): base the profile wherever he actually works from. Win the other market through the website alone until there's a real office there.

## 7. Compliance checklist (applies to every page and draft)

- **Medicare pages:**
  - Carry the TPMO disclaimer, filled in with Anthony's real numbers.
  - Under the CY2027 rule (in effect for materials since Oct 1, 2026), the wording points to Medicare.gov / 1-800-MEDICARE and no longer to the local SHIP. Get the exact wording from Anthony's upline or carriers before publishing (verify). The current draft is: "We do not offer every plan available in your area. Currently we represent [X] organizations which offer [Y] products in your area. Please contact Medicare.gov or 1-800-MEDICARE to get information on all of your options."
  - Imply no government affiliation; don't use "Medicare" as if it were the business name.
  - Don't promise "free" benefits or the "best plan."
  - The quote form only collects a callback request. The Scope of Appointment comes before any plan discussion.
- **Annuity pages:** use the existing disclosure line, make no return projections, and give no tax advice.
- **Everything:**
  - "Licensed in MO & OK."
  - No Texas or Illinois targeting.
  - No trusts, wills or estate planning.
  - The business name is always "Daniels' Legacy Planning"; the agent is "Anthony."

Frequently Asked Questions

Can life insurance help my family keep the house?

It can. If you pass away while the policy is in force, the benefit goes to the people you name, and they can use it toward the mortgage or anything else the family needs. We'll help you choose an amount and term that line up with your home loan and your budget.

What is final expense insurance?

It's a smaller whole life policy meant to help cover funeral, burial and other end-of-life costs. It's often chosen by older adults, or by grown children planning with a parent.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. The quote and the conversation about your options are free, and there's no obligation to buy anything. If you choose a policy, it has a premium, and coverage is subject to the insurance company's underwriting.

We live on the Illinois side of the metro. Can you help?

Not yet. We're licensed in Missouri and Oklahoma, so we can quote families in St. Louis City, St. Louis County and the rest of Missouri, but not Illinois residents right now.

What happens after I take the Legacy Check?

You'll get a text from us. Reply YES and we'll call you at the time you picked. From there a licensed DLP advisor walks through your options with you. No pressure, and you decide what's right for your family.

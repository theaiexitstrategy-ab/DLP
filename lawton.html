For Military Families

Already Have SGLI? Here's What Happens to It After You Leave the Service.

Servicemembers' Group Life Insurance doesn't simply follow you into civilian life. Here's the general timeline, so you can make a calm, informed decision.

After separation

SGLI Keeps Going for 120 Days

Your SGLI coverage generally continues for 120 days after you separate, at no cost to you. After that, it ends unless you've made other arrangements.

Your options

You Can Convert to VGLI

Veterans' Group Life Insurance lets you keep coverage after service. If you apply within 240 days of separating, you generally won't need to answer health questions. You can still apply up to 1 year and 120 days after separating, but health questions may apply then.

Worth knowing

VGLI Premiums Rise With Age

VGLI rates are set by age group and increase as you get older. Many veterans like to see how VGLI compares with private coverage before choosing what's right for their family.
